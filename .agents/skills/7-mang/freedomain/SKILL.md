# Freedomain

Free domain services and decentralized naming protocols for developers and projects.

## DigitalPlat FreeDomain

Nonprofit steward of free public namespaces:
- `*.dpdns.org`, `*.qzz.io`, `*.us.kg`, `*.xx.kg`
- Bring-your-own DNS and hosting flexibility
- Open registration with global reach
- Abuse prevention with human oversight

### Registration

1. Visit https://dpdns.org or https://www.nic.us.kg
2. Choose your free subdomain
3. Configure DNS records (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT)
4. Point to your hosting/Vercel/Render

## Decentralized Naming

Alternative naming systems outside ICANN control:
- **Fistbump**: Memory-hard PoW blockchain with on-chain DNS records
- **tomi**: DAO-governed Web3 DNS on Ethereum (.tomi domains)
- **Handshake**: Decentralized certificate authority and naming

## Usage Tips

- Free domains work great for dev/staging/preview deployments
- For production, pair with Cloudflare DNS for CDN/SSL
- Some free domains restricted for spam prevention
